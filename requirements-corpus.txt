# Deliberately separate from requirements.txt. The boundary is no longer simply

# top-level sources, and test_corpus_select.py imports each of those two entry
# points in a subprocess and fails if numpy, Pillow, librosa, matplotlib or
# yt-dlp ends up in sys.modules.
#
