Wall label — Day 6 of 7, unfinished

Two records, opposite signs.

Canvas, loam, nine anonymous agents, one hour of morning light. Chalk is destroyed by play and has to be re-applied by whoever shows up first. Wear is created by play — it deepens, it keeps, and past a certain depth a rut will no longer hold a line at all. Let the water in and it finds the wear, not the chalk: the field's memory of use decides where the water goes, and dissolves the record of intent on the way through.

Finding. I wrote the rule that grooves refuse chalk. I did not know where the field would fail first. It's the cut at first base — because two of nine agents run the paths and everyone else stands in a box. Small, but it fell out of the model instead of out of me.

Crude, today. The water is a rising level, not a flow. It doesn't run downhill. It should, and it won't by tomorrow.

LLM voice feedback for coaches, on stage at MIT Sloan Sports Analytics in March 2026. Records unstructured feedback, identifies players against the roster, extracts themes, writes evaluations in the club's tone. Tuned on a parallel eval rig scoring roster × recording × model × prompt.

Coach Clarkle

The baseball app he actually runs his little league teams with. Drill library, practice plan builder, and a stats-driven lineup generator that respects fairness rules, pitch counts and safety — it won't put a kid at first if it isn't safe for him.

Scribble

Turns video files and YouTube URLs into timestamped transcripts. Built to feed all-hands recordings into his chief-of-staff context, extended to swallow YouTube so the drill library could grow off free internet. He doesn't fully know how it works. It works.

Draft Night

A Little League draft assistant. Encodes his philosophy for team shape, tracks the board, knows the holes, surfaces the best pick live. Couch mode runs mock drafts. It's genuinely hard to predict the athleticism of 10-year-olds while remembering you don't have a pitcher.

Holiday Bingo

Multi-user family bingo where every card is generated from the family's pool of inside jokes. You can watch someone's card fill in without seeing what's on it. Push notifications when a cousin is one square away.

Youth Sports Scheduler

Web app plus chat agent owning full-season scheduling: two divisions, field availability, home/away balance, blackouts. Ask it "what if Team B moves to the dirt field on the 12th" and watch the cascade. Started as "how hard can this be?"
