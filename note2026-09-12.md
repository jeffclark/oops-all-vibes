Rib cookoff went off with a small hitch: we couldn’t keep up with demand! We tried a new strategy this year: we attempted 125 racks vs the 80 from last year (with the same equipment), and second is that we tried a different cooking method pulling them off when they hit temp before, then holding them to rest warmly for a while before putting them back on the smoker to finish later. This caused a gigantic line at our tent. Which more people joined because they were like “hey, there’s a line, it must be good!”. Some people waited in line for 40 minutes. We are not a well known BBQ team, so it was entirely because a line existed. I sincerely hope we didn’t let them down.

There are three ways to judge your accomplishments at the cookoff: first is existing in the first place, cooking with your friends in which is our primarily goal, we did and are happy with. Second is the official judging. We came in 8th. Which we’re fine with, we don’t really care. But the one that I really want is the people’s choice award. Every person entering the fairgrounds gets a little ticket. You go eat a bunch of ribs, and then drop your vote in the bucket on the table of your favorite place before you leave. I worked the table this year and was one of the faces of our team. We added a bunch of touches to try to win favor: new banners, a big bell people could ring when they voted, and hilariously a stack of trophies on the table. Not BBQ trophies like everyone else had (since we had one participation one) - but soccer trophies from the 90’s. So when people would wait in that long line, they’d tell me that it was because of the line and the crazy set of trophies. In which I’d say look closer, they’d realize they’re soccer trophies and laugh. That was awesome. We’ll find out who won the people’s choice later today. I don’t think we got it. Our line was massive - I think everyon came through our tent - but I don’t know that we got everyone to ring the bell. We were the last tent standing htis year though, which was another part of our strategy. But I still can’t remember a lot of people at the end dropping their ticket in our bucket. But they seemed happy. Who knows. We have work to do for next year. I’m good not looking at or smelling a rib for a couple of days regardless!
